Oyster Passport privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026

The Oyster Passport is an optional account on hsoysters.com for keeping track of the oysters you eat at Half Shell. Our menu works the same without one. This page explains what the passport stores, why, and how to see or delete it.

What we collect

  • Your phone number, to sign you in. We text you a code to check it’s your phone.
  • Your name and birthday, and your email if you give one.
  • Card digits, if you add a card: the last 4 digits and the card type. See Card digits.
  • What you log: the date of each visit, the oysters and how many, and any ratings, “two words” and notes you add; the stamps you earn.
  • The days you open our menu while signed in.

Why

  • To run your passport: your history, ratings, stamps and the suggestions based on them.
  • Your birthday: for a birthday reward, and for our age rules. You must be 13 or older to have a passport, and we show nothing that promotes alcohol to anyone under 21.
  • Card digits and the days you open our menu: to match your check to your passport, so the oysters you order can be added without you logging each one. We will match a check by the phone number on it, or by a card you added together with a day you opened our menu.

Card digits

Adding a card is optional, and you can remove it any time in your passport’s Profile.

  • We keep only the last 4 digits and the card type (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover or other). We never ask for or store the full card number, the expiry date or the security code, and we can’t charge a card with what we keep.
  • We use them only to match your check to your passport, as described above.
  • Paying with Apple Pay or Google Pay? Your Wallet app shows a different last 4 for that card. Use that one.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your information.
  • We don’t send marketing texts or emails.
  • We don’t share it, except with the Google services that run the passport (below).

Where it’s kept, and who can see it

Your passport is stored in Google Firebase, which also handles signing in by text. You can see your own passport. Half Shell’s site administrator can see passport records in our database to fix problems. Staff on the floor can’t see your passport.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see how the passport is used: for example, that someone signed up, logged an oyster (which oyster and how many), rated one (which oyster and how many stars), saved a visit, or earned a stamp. None of this includes your name, phone number, email, birthday, card digits, notes or account ID. On passport pages, Google signals and ad personalization are turned off. Our menu pages use Google Analytics for every visitor, signed in or not.

Seeing, changing and deleting your data

In your passport’s Profile you can:

  • change your name and email;
  • correct your birthday once;
  • add or remove cards;
  • edit or delete any visit (in History);
  • export your data: two files, everything in your passport (JSON) and your visits as a spreadsheet (CSV);
  • delete your account: this permanently deletes your passport, history, stamps, cards and sign-in. It can’t be undone.

Backups

We keep daily backups of our database in case something goes wrong. Each backup is deleted after 30 days, so after you delete your account, your data leaves our backups within 30 days.

Age

You must be 13 or older to create a passport. If someone under 13 tries, we don’t create the account and keep nothing they entered.
